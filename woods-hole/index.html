Getting there

A ride, a path, or the ferry lot

Woods Hole sits at the southwestern tip of Falmouth. These are the ways we can confirm today. Always check the official source before you travel, especially in summer.

A founding partner of this magazine, and a local guide to rides around Falmouth, including Woods Hole and the ferry terminal. Based at the Cape Island Café office, 316 Gifford Street.

Shining Sea Bikeway

A 10.7-mile paved path on the old railroad bed, from Woods Hole on Vineyard Sound to North Falmouth on Buzzards Bay. The Woods Hole stretch is on the pull-out map at Woods Hole side B3. The town maintains it for cycling, walking, jogging, skating, and cross-country skiing.

Find it Woods Hole side B3

Town of Falmouth, The Shining Sea Bikeway

Steamship Authority parking and shuttle

The Steamship Authority’s Woods Hole Terminal is at 1 Cowdry Road, Woods Hole, MA 02543. There is no dockside parking at the terminal. Travelers park in off-site lots in Falmouth, including the Palmer Avenue Lot at 286 Palmer Avenue, and take a free Steamship Authority shuttle to the terminal. The Palmer Avenue lot is marked on the pull-out map at Woods Hole side C1.

Lot status, rates, and shuttle times change. Check the Steamship Authority parking page before you go, or call the Woods Hole parking information line at (508) 457-7275. The terminal number is (508) 548-3788.

Find it Terminal, Woods Hole side A4 · Palmer Avenue lot, Woods Hole side C1

On the waterfront

The crossing between Woods Hole and Martha’s Vineyard is about 45 minutes, according to the Steamship Authority. Confirm today’s schedule at steamshipauthority.com.

WHOI and the Marine Biological Laboratory are working research campuses. Enjoy the village waterfront and official visitor information rather than walking into labs. The Woods Hole Science Aquarium at 166 Water Street is closed through early 2027 for repairs; check NOAA’s visit page for the reopening date.
