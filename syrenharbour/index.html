On the Water

Welcome to the harbour

Syren Harbour Bar & Restaurant sits right on the water at 71 Water St, on the shores of Eel Pond in the village of Woods Hole. We’re putting the finishing touches on the dining room now, and we’re looking forward to sharing our table with you: good company, thoughtful plates and the quiet charm of the water.

Our menu is built around island flavors, from jerk chicken and mojo criollo shrimp to empanadas, wings and personal pizzas.

Our Food

Island flavors, harbour views

Soups from our kitchen

Warm up with our Chicken Pumpkin soup, made with no spice, or a lightly spiced Shrimp Butternut Bisque.

Mains with a Caribbean heart

Our Haka style Jerk Chicken is an herb and spice steeped leg quarter, battered and fried, then served with rice and peas, steamed cabbage, sweet plantain and Tamarind sauce. Mojo Criollo Shrimp brings jumbo shrimp sautéed in a savory citrus orange, lime and garlic sauce, served with a Corn and Carrot Yellow Rice Risotto. The Porchetta Pernil is seasoned Dominican Pernil style and comes with garlic green beans, Yucca Mofongo and sweet plantain. The Shrimp Milanesa is a shrimp cutlet fried crisp and topped with a fresh arugula salad.

Starters made for sharing

Choose Crispy Wings in five styles, from sweet Tamarind and savory Mojo Criollo to our Syren Dry Rub, Scotch Bonnet Honey and Jerk Apricot. There are beef and chicken Empanadas, Popcorn Shrimp with tartar or garlic aioli, Vermont Brussels glazed with Vermont Maple Mustard Vinaigrette, a Caribbean Caesar with adobo limón dressing, and a Jerk Chicken Arugula Flat Bread with Scotch Bonnet Honey.

Woods Hole

In the heart of the village

Woods Hole is a working harbour village of ferries, fishing boats and leading ocean science institutions. From 71 Water St, most of it is a few minutes’ walk away.

  • Eel Pond & the Drawbridge

    Right outside

    Eel Pond, its moored boats and the little drawbridge on Water Street are part of the view.

  • Marine Biological Laboratory

    About 3 min walk

    The MBL campus sits just down Water Street.

  • WHOI Ocean Science Discovery Center

    About 3 min walk

    Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution’s visitor center on School Street.

  • Woods Hole Science Aquarium

    About 5 min walk

    NOAA’s aquarium at the end of Water Street, established in 1875. It is closed for renovations through early 2027, so check NOAA before you go.

  • Steamship Authority Ferry

    About 6 min walk

    Year-round ferries between Woods Hole and Martha’s Vineyard leave from the terminal on Railroad Avenue.

  • Shining Sea Bikeway

    About 6 min walk

    The 10.7-mile paved path to North Falmouth starts at the Steamship Authority terminal.

  • Nobska Light

    About 1.2 mi

    The lighthouse overlooking Vineyard Sound makes a good walk or ride before dinner.

Walking times are approximate and measured from 71 Water St.
